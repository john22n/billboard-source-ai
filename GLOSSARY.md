# Billboard Source AI notifications

Notification vocabulary complements the existing sales and call-intake language in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md).

## Language

**Nutshell Web Lead**:
A Nutshell sales opportunity originating through Contact Us Page. For this notification feature, other intake sources are excluded; it is distinct from the call-intake Lead captured in Billboard Source AI.
_Avoid_: Lead (without context)

**Lead Notification**:
A persistent notice to the assigned Sales Rep about a Nutshell Web Lead. It remains active until that representative explicitly clears it, including across sign-outs.
_Avoid_: Desktop banner

**Notification Acknowledgment**:
The representative's explicit Seen/Clear action on a Lead Notification. It does not establish customer contact or change the Nutshell lead's status.
_Avoid_: Contacted, resolved lead

**Notification Urgency**:
The age category of an active Lead Notification measured from the Nutshell Web Lead's creation, including evenings and weekends: green below one hour, yellow from one through four hours, and red above four hours.
_Avoid_: Contact SLA, response time
